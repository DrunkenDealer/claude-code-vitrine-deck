Vitrine background library
=========================

Photography sourced from Pexels (https://www.pexels.com) under the Pexels
License — https://www.pexels.com/license/

The Pexels License grants free use for commercial and non-commercial purposes
with no attribution required. The photographers are credited here anyway, and
in assets/backgrounds/index.json, because a bundled art library should say
where its art came from.

Changed from the originals: each photo is cropped to a 1500x2600 portrait frame,
and again to a panorama frame of up to 6000x3900 (wide/; smaller where
the original is, never enlarged), and re-encoded as WebP at quality 76. No
other edit is made — the colour and composition are the photographer's.

These files are backgrounds a deck composites its own artwork over. They are not
redistributed as stock photography and are not offered for download as
standalone images.
